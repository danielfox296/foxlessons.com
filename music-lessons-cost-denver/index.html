How much do music lessons cost in Denver?

The straight answer, with the market table. I publish my own rates, so I can publish everyone's.

The short answer.

As of August 2026, a 60-minute private lesson in Denver runs about $60 to $70 at budget studios, $76 to $88 at the standard academies, $70 to $80 with independent single-instrument specialists, and $90 to $130 for premium and in-home teaching. Big-box programs price differently: Guitar Center works out to about $55 an hour plus a registration fee, and School of Rock bundles lessons with a band program at roughly $250 to $355 a month.

My rates sit in the premium in-home band and are published in full: $90 an hour in your home, $75 on Zoom, kids $70 per 45 minutes, with founding rates lower through 2026. The grid is one page. Now here's everyone else.

Reading the table

What actually moves the price.

Travel. In-home lessons cost more everywhere they're offered, because the driving is part of the lesson. DaVinci adds a monthly in-home fee, RockHome's whole model is in-home, and my in-home rate is $15 over my Zoom rate for the same reason.

Fees and terms. A registration fee, an auto-renewing monthly pack, or a 30-day cancellation notice changes what a listed price really costs. Before you compare two numbers, ask three questions: is there a signup fee, what happens to missed lessons, and how do you stop. My answers: no fee, 24 hours' notice credits the lesson forward, and you stop anytime.

Who teaches you. At the big boxes, part of your fee runs the building and the brand. With any solo teacher, me included, the whole fee is the person in the room with you. Neither is wrong. They're different products.

The big boxes, briefly

Guitar Center, Music & Arts, School of Rock.

Guitar Center runs lessons at three metro stores. As of August 2026, $109 a month for four 30-minute lessons or $219 for four 60-minute lessons, plus a $30 registration fee, on a 28-day auto-renew. The full comparison is its own page.

Music & Arts, owned by Guitar Center, has stores in Littleton, Lone Tree, Westminster, and Englewood, none in Denver proper. Lessons run $26 to $49 per half hour plus the $30 registration. Their band-and-orchestra rental counter is genuinely useful: if your kid needs a school-band instrument, renting there is the right move, whoever teaches them.

School of Rock blankets the metro and bundles private lessons with a real band program at roughly $250 to $355 a month plus enrollment and show fees. For a kid who wants a band, it's a genuine offer. The honest comparison is its own page too.
