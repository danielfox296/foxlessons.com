The promise

What lessons will do for you.

You'll make significant improvements in playing, enjoying, and understanding music, from chord charts and by ear. And you'll feel great about doing it, whether you play by yourself or for your friends. That's the promise I make every student.

How we get there is built around you. We work on real songs you're actually interested in, immediately. I bring in exercises only as far as they serve those songs. And you learn to move forward on your own: how to decipher the chords in a chart, and how to understand by ear what's going on. I don't teach sight-reading, and you won't need it.

Where it can go

Some students have a destination, and some find one on the way.

Maybe you already know what you want from this: a song for somebody's wedding, playing with other people, an album of your own. If you can't name it yet, here's what students do with lessons. None of it is required, and playing at home for yourself is a complete destination.

It can be a Denver jam night, prepped ahead of time, with me there; a recorded track of you playing, made in Logic; playing for your family; or finishing a song of your own. The adults page walks through each one.
