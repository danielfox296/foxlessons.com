From scrap to demo.

Songwriting lessons cover the whole path: the writing itself, lyrics, editing, the functional harmony that makes chords do what your song needs, performing it, and recording a demo of it in Logic. You can enter anywhere on that path. A voice memo full of hooks counts. So does a finished lyric with no music, or a chord loop with no words.

The demo matters. Most people have never heard their own song played back at them, finished. We finish things here: a recorded demo you can keep, share, or build on.

How it runs

Your material, your genre.

We work on your songs, in the style you actually write in. Functional harmony gets taught inside them: why that chord lands, what the five chord is doing, what to reach for when the bridge sags. Same rule as everything I teach: theory only as far as it serves the song.

If you want to perform your songs, we work on performing them, playing and singing for people in whatever room that means for you. And if the recording side is the pull, Logic Pro lessons go deeper on tracking and mixing. The two lanes meet at your demo.
