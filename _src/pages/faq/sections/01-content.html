FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

How much do lessons cost?

For adults, $90 an hour in your home and $75 on Zoom. Kids run 45 minutes in home at $70. Founding rates for my first 10 new students, through December 31, 2026: $75 in home, $60 on Zoom, $60 for kids. The full grid is on the rates page.

How do I book a first lesson?

Start on the booking page. Pick your lesson, we lock a time, and the first lesson is paid up front. It credits toward your first month.

Where do lessons happen?

I come to you in the Denver metro, we meet at the studio, or we work over video. You pick when we set the slot.

Am I too old to start?

No. Adults starting from zero and adults coming back after twenty years away are exactly who these lessons are built for. You'll play music at the first lesson, whatever age you bring to it.

How long until I can play a real song?

You'll play something at the first lesson. A song you'd play for someone else takes weeks of a held weekly slot, and the honest answer depends on the song. I'll give you that answer once I've heard you play.

What do I need?

Your instrument and whatever you've been playing or trying to play. For production lessons, a laptop with Logic Pro X. If you don't own an instrument yet, say so in your note and I'll point you at the right first one for your budget.

What if I have to cancel?

Give me 24 hours' notice and the lesson credits forward in full.

Do you teach kids?

Yes, in shorter slots. Guitar, piano, and drums are the usual starting points.

Do lessons work over video?

Yes. Production translates over screen share especially well, and instrument lessons run over video too. Where it's practical, a first lesson or two in person helps me hear you accurately.
