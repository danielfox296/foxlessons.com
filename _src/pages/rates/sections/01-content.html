What the rate includes

More than the hour.

A held weekly slot that's yours. My notes after every lesson, and for kids, notes the parent sees. Lesson recordings, on request. A midweek check-in by text or call when you want it: accountability, hard spots, quick answers on the tune if I know it off the top of my head. No registration fee, ever.

Founding students also get direct text access to me between lessons. That's part of the founding package.

How paying works

Monthly, month to month.

Your first lesson is paid up front when you book, and it credits toward your first month. If the first lesson isn't a fit, it's free.

After that, lessons bill monthly, month to month, stop anytime. Card, Venmo, or Zelle. Cancel any lesson with 24 hours' notice and it credits forward in full.
