Bass is my first instrument.

I've played it longer than anything else I do. Electric and upright, on stages and in studios, with western jazz theory underneath the whole thing. Most of what I understand about music arrived through the bass, and teaching it is the closest thing I have to giving directions in my own neighborhood.

Lessons meet you where you are. Starting from zero, we build time, tone, and your first songs. Coming back after years away, we find what's still in your hands and go from there. Gigging and stuck, we name the wall and take it apart. Reading, walking lines, groove construction, locking with a drummer, all of it is on the table, in whatever order your playing calls for.

Theory, when you want it

You'll know why the line works.

The theory here is western jazz theory. In practice that means you won't just copy bass lines. You'll understand what a line is doing to the song, and you'll build your own. We use it in service of the music you're playing, at whatever dose you want.
