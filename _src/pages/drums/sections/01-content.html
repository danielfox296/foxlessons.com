Time first.

Drum lessons start with groove. A drummer who keeps honest time and makes a band feel good is worth more than one with fast hands, so that's the order we work in: time, feel, dynamics, then vocabulary. You add the flashy stuff fast once the pocket is real.

We work on a kit if you have one, and on a pad and a plan if you don't yet. Beginners and returning players both fit, and kids do well here in shorter slots.
